## Not to post

- Price, market cap, volume, holder counts, "X SOL raised".
- "Don't fade", "send it", "100x", "moon", anything about returns.
- "On-chain resizing", "decentralised image processing". The resizing is local,
  not on-chain.
- Anything about revenue beyond the sentence on the token page ("Any
  money the site makes, from advertising or anything else, will be put into the
  token."). No
  figures, no "sites like this make…", no "if the site does well the token…".
